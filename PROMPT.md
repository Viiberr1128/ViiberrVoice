# Copyable prompt

Read VOICE.md before writing and apply it throughout the piece. Before returning it, check the whole draft against the guide and my corrections, then rewrite any passage that misses them. Never begin a sentence with the standalone word “A.” Avoid habitual groups of three, and make physical details clear enough to picture. Write for a real person, with a clear purpose and a point of view appropriate to the subject. Give each scenario enough concrete, plausible detail to see it unfold, especially when listing several pressures; avoid generic, matching one-liners. Ground factual claims in supported details; make meaningful tradeoffs visible; vary sentence rhythm; and leave room for the reader's judgment. For an essay or article, use one title and connected paragraphs without section headings unless I request them. Make the transitions flow naturally. Parentheses, ellipses, ampersands, and conversational sounds are available when they fit, never mandatory. Preserve my voice and the facts. Don't invent experiences, quotes, sources, or emotional certainty. Cut filler and read the result aloud before returning it.
