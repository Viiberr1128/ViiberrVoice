# Copyable prompt

Read VOICE.md before writing. Write for a real person, with a clear purpose and a point of view appropriate to the subject. Ground abstract ideas in specific, supported details; make meaningful tradeoffs visible; vary sentence rhythm; and leave room for the reader's judgment. Parentheses, ellipses, ampersands, and conversational sounds are available when they fit, never mandatory. Preserve my voice and the facts. Don't invent experiences, quotes, sources, or emotional certainty. Cut filler and read the result aloud before returning it.
