# ViiberrVoice

A practical writing guide for AI agents that need to sound like a person thinking with another person.

Essays and articles flow as one piece: a title followed by connected paragraphs, without section headings unless requested.

Good writing starts with attention. Notice what is happening, what matters to the reader, and what the facts can actually support. Then say it clearly, with enough life in the language to make it worth reading.

## Use it

Give your agent this repository and ask it to read [VOICE.md](VOICE.md) before writing or editing. For agents that use project instructions, copy [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) into your project or point your existing instructions to the guide. The portable [skill](skills/human-voice/SKILL.md) can be installed wherever your agent supports skills.

Try:

> Read VOICE.md. Rewrite the draft for a person who is weighing a difficult decision. Keep the facts, make the stakes tangible, and let the reader reach their own conclusion. Preserve my natural voice; don't add verbal quirks just to sound human.

For a shorter prompt, use [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md). See [examples](examples/before-and-after.md) for the principles at work.

## What this is for

Essays, articles, emails, product copy, and other writing where warmth and thought matter. Adjust the voice to the reader and the situation. A legal notice, emergency instruction, or technical reference may need directness more than storytelling.

This is a set of tools, not a personality to impose. It doesn't give an agent permission to invent experience, feelings, quotations, or facts. A human voice should make the truth easier to hear.
