# Before & after

These examples are invented to show how the principles work. Details in illustrative copy are not claims about a real person or product.

## From abstraction to a real tradeoff

**Before:** A flexible schedule can improve work-life balance and support employee well-being.

**After:** A flexible schedule can make room for a school pickup or a doctor's appointment. It can also blur the end of the workday if everyone is expected to be available at all hours. The policy matters; so does the way people are allowed to use it.

The second version gives the reader something to picture and names a real tension. In an actual policy, confirm the details before presenting them as fact.

## A small aside

**Before:** Moving requires significant preparation and can present unexpected challenges.

**After:** Moving means finding boxes, changing your address, and figuring out where the kettle went (usually when you need it most).

The aside makes a mundane moment recognizable. It would be out of place in an urgent evacuation notice.

## Space for mixed feelings

**Before:** Selling your business is an exciting opportunity to start a new chapter.

**After:** Selling a business can make financial sense and still feel like saying goodbye to something you built. Both can be true. The price matters, and so do the terms, the people involved, and what you want your days to look like afterward.

The revision doesn't tell the reader to celebrate or grieve. It gives them room to decide what matters.

## Restraint

**Overdone:** Well... life is messy (isn't it?!), and sometimes—oof!—you just have to embrace the journey & trust the process...

**Better:** The plan changed. That doesn't erase the work you put into it.

The second version has a voice because it has a clear thought. It doesn't need extra punctuation to prove a human is present.

## Let each situation unfold

**Before:** The roof needs work. The business is struggling. The bills keep coming.

**After:** A storm pulled shingles from the roof last summer, and now a bucket comes out whenever the rain gets heavy. The business is running on a shoestring budget that somehow keeps getting tighter. And every trip to the mailbox seems to bring another bill asking to be paid before the next check comes in.

The details make each pressure visible and give the sentences room to breathe. This is an imagined scenario; don't present its details as facts about an actual person without support.
