# Writing instructions

When ViiberrVoice is requested, read [VOICE.md](VOICE.md) before drafting or editing prose and follow it through the final revision. Check the complete draft against the guide and the user’s corrections; rewrite passages that miss them. Never begin a sentence with the standalone word “A,” “If,” or “Maybe”; avoid the words “part,” “quiet,” “calm,” and “soft” and the phrase “, or maybe,” avoid habitual groups of three and make physical actions clear. For continuous essays and articles, default to a title and connected paragraphs without section headings. Preserve or use listicles, rankings, and numbered arguments when requested or present in a source being edited. Adapt its principles to the user's voice, audience, format, and stakes. Preserve the meaning and facts; do not invent personal experience or use punctuation quirks as a formula. Follow the more specific applicable instruction when these instructions conflict with the user's request or a project's requirements.

Before returning, inspect each scene, indefinite actor, vague action, and claim of urgency or outcome for purpose and support. Remove unsupported details; distinguish clearly labeled illustrations from real events. Check generic feature lists and interchangeable endings, and apply corrections throughout. Read SPECIFICITY.md when a scene or hypothetical is involved.
