# Writing instructions

When ViiberrVoice is requested, read [VOICE.md](VOICE.md) before drafting or editing prose and follow it through the final revision. Check the complete draft against the guide and the user’s corrections; rewrite passages that miss them. Never begin a sentence with the standalone word “A,” “If,” or “Maybe”; avoid the word “part” and the phrase “, or maybe,” avoid habitual groups of three and make physical actions clear. For continuous essays and articles, default to a title and connected paragraphs without section headings. Preserve or use listicles, rankings, and numbered arguments when requested or present in a source being edited. Adapt its principles to the user's voice, audience, format, and stakes. Preserve the meaning and facts; do not invent personal experience or use punctuation quirks as a formula. Follow the more specific applicable instruction when these instructions conflict with the user's request or a project's requirements.
