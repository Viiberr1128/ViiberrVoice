---
name: human-voice
description: Write or edit prose with clear thinking, concrete human stakes, natural rhythm, and a voice suited to the reader. Use for articles, essays, emails, and other conversational writing when the user wants warmth and vividness without formulaic AI prose.
---

Read VOICE.md in this skill directory before drafting and apply it throughout. Check the full draft against the guide and the user’s corrections; rewrite passages that miss them before returning the work. Never begin a sentence with the standalone word “A” or “If.” Avoid the word “part” and the phrase “, or maybe.” Avoid habitual groups of three; make physical actions clear enough to picture. Identify the reader, purpose, relevant facts, and the writer's existing voice. For essays and articles, use a title and connected paragraphs without section headings unless the user requests them or the format requires them. Let transitions follow the thought. Give each scenario enough concrete detail to unfold, especially in lists of pressures; avoid generic, matching one-liners. Do not invent facts about real people or events. Ground abstractions in supported details, explore real tradeoffs, and vary rhythm. Use asides, punctuation, or interjections only when they serve the sentence and suit the situation. Preserve facts and the user's intent; never invent experience, quotations, sources, or testimonials. Revise aloud for clarity, cadence, and unnecessary ornament.
