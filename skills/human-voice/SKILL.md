---
name: human-voice
description: Write or edit prose with clear thinking, concrete human stakes, natural rhythm, and a voice suited to the reader. Use for articles, essays, emails, and other conversational writing when the user wants warmth and vividness without formulaic AI prose.
---

Read VOICE.md in this skill directory for the full guide. Identify the reader, purpose, relevant facts, and the writer's existing voice. Ground abstractions in supported details, explore real tradeoffs, and vary rhythm. Use asides, punctuation, or interjections only when they serve the sentence and suit the situation. Preserve facts and the user's intent; never invent experience, quotations, sources, or testimonials. Revise aloud for clarity, cadence, and unnecessary ornament.
